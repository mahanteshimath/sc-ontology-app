AMENDMENT No. 2 to Supply Agreement SCA-0042-2024
Between Buyer and Supplier 0042 Materials (APAC)
Effective: October 1, 2026

The parties agree that the following clauses replace the corresponding clauses of the
original agreement in their entirety.

Clause 4.1 - Delivery performance.
Supplier shall deliver no fewer than 87% of purchase order lines on or before the confirmed
delivery date. Performance is measured across all purchase order lines received, each line
counting equally.

Clause 5 - Service credits.
For any shortfall against clause 4.1, Supplier shall credit Buyer 4% of the invoiced value of
each late line, not to exceed USD 100,000 per contract year.

Clause 7 - Commercial terms.
Standard lead time is 30 calendar days from order acknowledgement. Payment terms remain Net 60.
Goods are delivered DAP (Incoterms 2020).

All other terms of the agreement remain unchanged.
